The challenge

Academic quality is never finished. It has to be reviewed, renewed and evidenced.

Programmes age, cohorts change, and the expectations of employers, regulators and learners keep moving. Institutions that hold their standards review academic practice as a routine part of running programmes. Six areas need that attention.

01

Curriculum

Content, outcomes and sequencing that stay current with the discipline and the profession it serves.

Is the programme still preparing graduates for the field they will enter?

02

Teaching & Learning

Pedagogy and delivery, face-to-face, online or blended, that engage the learners actually in the room.

Are teaching methods matched to the outcomes we promise?

03

Assessment

Assessment that is valid, reliable and fair, moderated consistently and protected by sound integrity processes.

Do our grades mean the same thing across cohorts and assessors?

04

Faculty Capability

Teaching teams supported to develop their practice, with mentoring and leadership pathways that last.

How do we grow capability beyond a one-off workshop?

05

Academic Quality

Frameworks, programme review and governance that make standards visible and defensible.

Would our programmes stand up to external review today?

06

Institutional Practice

Policy, planning and leadership that connect academic decisions to institutional direction.

Are our academic policies being applied as written?

Elevate Academia works alongside institutions to review these areas, strengthen what is working and improve what is not, with practical solutions academic teams can carry forward.

Our approach

A consistent methodology, adapted to each institution.

Every engagement follows the same discipline: understand the context before recommending anything, and leave behind practice that continues to improve once the engagement ends.

Founder

Pavi Dhamija

Founder · Academic & Education Consultant

Pavi founded Elevate Academia to bring the practical knowledge of working inside academic institutions to the institutions that need it.

She is a strategic academic with 12 years of industry knowledge and hands-on education delivery experience, across health and wellbeing, sports and exercise science, sterilisation and infection risk management, adult teaching and accreditation project management.

She spent a decade in the Faculty of Health and Education at Toi Ohomai Institute of Technology in New Zealand, including as Academic Lead, before moving into programme development at Te Whare Wānanga o Awanuiārangi. In Canada she now serves on the Academic Board of Native Tech Indigenous Academy and writes curriculum for the Saskatchewan Indian Institute of Technology.
